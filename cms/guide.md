# Welcome to your site editor

Everything on your page can be changed right here. Nothing goes live until you click **Save**, and after you save, your site updates on its own in about two minutes. Refresh your site to see the change.

This guide always lives at the top of the menu, so you never have to go looking for it.

## What's in the menu

- **Start here:** this guide.
- **Your page:** every word, photo and button on your website. The sections are numbered 1 to 13 in the same order you see them on the page, top to bottom.
- **Site settings:** your booking link, email address, Facebook link, the words in the menu, and the small print in the footer. Change one of these once and every spot on the page that uses it updates.

## Changing words

1. Open **Your page**.
2. Open the section by its number (for example, **6. Packages**).
3. Click into a box and type.
4. Click **Save**.

The gray text under a box tells you anything special about it.

## Little formatting tricks

- **Coral highlight:** put two stars around words, like `**ACTION!**`. This works in the headline at the top, the How to get noticed heading, the stats heading and paragraph, and the big navy breaks.
- **Coral underline:** put two underscores around words, like `__Pinterest is a search engine__`. This works in the Why Pinterest text.
- **New paragraph:** leave an empty line between paragraphs.
- **The big italic questions at the top:** any paragraph in the top section that ends with a question mark gets that style automatically.

## Lists: packages, questions, pins, stats and more

- **Add one:** use the add button at the end of the list.
- **Remove one:** use the delete button on that item.
- **Change the order:** drag the item up or down. The page shows them in the same order.
- On a package, **Tab above the card** adds the little tab (like "New to Pinterest"), and **Tab color** picks navy or coral.

## Photos and pins

- Click a photo box to upload a new picture or pick one you already uploaded.
- Use JPG, PNG or WebP. Keep each one under about 500 KB so your page stays fast. Phone photos are often 3 to 5 MB, so shrink them first at squoosh.app (free).
- Pin examples look best tall, in the 2:3 Pinterest shape, like 1000 x 1500.
- Always fill in the description box under a photo. It's what screen readers say out loud, and Google reads it too.

## Your booking buttons

Every booking button on the page uses the booking link in **Site settings**. If your Acuity link ever changes, change it there once and every button updates.

Where a button link box says `BOOKING_URL`, that simply means "use my booking link." You can also paste any other web address there instead.

## Your email list

Open **Your page**, then **Email list**.

- The switch shows or hides both email list buttons (the one at the top of every screen and the one on the last screen).
- When your email platform is ready, paste your signup link in **Signup link**. It has to be a page people can open, often called a share link, form link or landing page. Embed code won't work here.
- While the link box is empty, the buttons scroll down to the last screen instead.

## How you show up in Google

The first two boxes in **Your page** are your **Title in Google** (the blue link people click, best under 60 characters) and your **Description in Google** (the gray text under it, around 150 characters).

## Good to know

- Some boxes can't be left empty, and the editor won't save until they're filled in. That's on purpose, so a blank box can never break your page.
- You can't permanently break anything. Every save is kept, and any earlier version can be brought back.
- The look of your site (colors, fonts and layout) isn't in the editor on purpose, so it always stays on brand.
